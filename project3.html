Traditional Uses, Phytochemistry, and Pharmacological Properties of Eclipta alba (Linn.) Hassk

Home / Projects / Project Details

Project Overview

This comprehensive review article explores Eclipta alba (Linn.) Hassk, commonly known as Bhringraj, a medicinal plant widely used in traditional Ayurvedic and Unani medicine systems. The review systematically documents its traditional uses, phytochemical constituents, and scientifically validated pharmacological activities.

Introduction

Eclipta alba belongs to the Asteraceae family and is distributed throughout tropical and subtropical regions. It has been traditionally used for hair growth, liver disorders, skin diseases, and as a general tonic. Modern research has begun to validate many of these traditional claims through phytochemical and pharmacological studies.

Traditional Uses

  • Hair Care: Used as a natural remedy for hair loss, premature graying, and scalp health
  • Liver Health: Traditionally prescribed for jaundice and liver detoxification
  • Skin Disorders: Applied for eczema, dermatitis, and wound healing
  • General Tonic: Used to improve overall vitality and immunity
  • Snake Bite: Used in traditional antivenom preparations

Phytochemistry

The plant contains a rich diversity of bioactive compounds including:

  1. Coumestans: Wedelolactone and demethylwedelolactone - major bioactive compounds
  2. Flavonoids: Apigenin, luteolin, and their glycosides
  3. Alkaloids: Ecliptine and nicotine
  4. Triterpenoids: Eclalbatin, ursolic acid, and oleanolic acid
  5. Thiophene Derivatives: Polyacetylenic compounds with antimicrobial activity

Pharmacological Properties

Scientific studies have demonstrated numerous pharmacological activities:

  • Hepatoprotective: Protects against liver damage induced by toxins
  • Antimicrobial: Shows activity against various bacteria and fungi
  • Anti-inflammatory: Reduces inflammation through multiple pathways
  • Anticancer: Demonstrates cytotoxic effects against cancer cell lines
  • Hair Growth Promoting: Stimulates follicle development and pigmentation

Conclusion

Eclipta alba represents a valuable medicinal plant with significant therapeutic potential. The review highlights the need for further clinical studies to fully establish its efficacy and safety profile for modern medical applications.
